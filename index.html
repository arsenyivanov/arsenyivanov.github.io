Bio

I am a Generative AI Research Scientist at AXXX. I work on diffusion language models as a fast, controllable, and principled alternative to standard autoregressive LLMs. More broadly, I am interested in the foundations of generative modeling and optimal transport, especially for discrete data such as text, DNA, and proteins. I also work on reinforcement learning and stochastic optimal control for generative models, and on sampling from unnormalized densities.

I hold a Master's degree with honors in Math of Machine Learning from HSE University. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at NUST MISIS. I also have three years of industry experience in machine learning research, mostly on graph neural networks, recommender systems, and multimodal large language models for robotics.

The complete list of my works can be found in my Google Scholar profile.
